## Translate the notation before combining formulas

| Review notation | Convention used here |
|---|---|
| $J_z=\sum_i j_z^{(i)}$, generator $A$ | $H=\sum_i Z_i/2$; the same spin normalization |
| $\varrho$, $f(x;\theta)$, $F(\theta)$ | $\rho$, $P_\theta(x)$, classical information $F_C(\theta)$ |
| $F_Q[\varrho,A]$ | QFI of the family generated by $H$ |
| $M$ as a measured observable | $M$ counts sensors in one GHZ group; $Y$ is its decoded parity |
| $L$ as the symmetric logarithmic derivative | $L$ denotes lattice side length here; read symbols in their local context |
| Noise probability $p$ in Eq. (132) | Here $p$ is a terminal assignment-error probability |
| Metrological parameter $\chi^2$ in Eq. (102) | Here $\chi$ is a detector-discrimination exponent |

The project writes the GHZ relative phase as $+M\theta$; the sign displayed in the review's Eq. (27) is opposite. Reversing the phase orientation leaves the cosine fringe and Fisher information unchanged. Keep the known offset $\phi$ consistent with the chosen orientation.

## What the bridge must add

The experimental order here is **supplied probe → additive signal → depth-limited coherent readout → independent terminal detectors**. Its depth restriction is a restriction on the receiver. Moving detector noise through an entangling readout generally changes the channel and its correlations. The review's noisy-encoding formulas therefore do not by themselves establish this project's detector-depth bound.

The [bridge](BRIDGE.md) supplies the missing steps: explicit noisy likelihoods, protected block parity, labeled settings, the erasure comparison, causal-support covariance, and the assembly of finite blocks into the frontier. The full proofs remain in [science/](../science/README.md). In particular, the environmental gauge is a research-level argument with an [identified primary dependency](../references/RELATED_WORK.md#zhou2021), even though its operational motivation can be explained simply.
